## Prompts finales

**Umbral** — Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide panoramic fashion campaign photograph for a premium independent underground clothing label. A model wearing oversized distressed charcoal denim and washed black layers, moving through a concrete underpass at dusk. Analog editorial photography, subtle flash, granular texture, confident candid attitude. Very wide horizontal 3:2 composition with the full figure around the central third and substantial architectural environment on both sides, suitable for slicing into six narrow vertical strips. No lettering, logo, watermark, border, collage or catalog backdrop.
